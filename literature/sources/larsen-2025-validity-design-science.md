## Key Findings
- Abstract: "validity is neither well-understood nor consistently established in design science … As a result, it is challenging to demonstrate and communicate the validity of knowledge claims about artifacts."
- Literature review: "We found a lack of consistency and a lack of actual use of validity terms in design science papers … Once researchers commit to shared validity norms, communication and reporting consistency should improve."

## Relevance
Paper 1 S2-2: a peer-reviewed gap statement for design science, about how its knowledge claims are verified.

## Caveats
- It proposes its own Design Science Validity Framework: an author proposal, not a consensus guideline. Cite it for the gap it states, not as evidence that no framework exists.
- Tier **A**.
