## Summary
Proposes R2C (Retrieval-Augmented Reasoning Consistency), an uncertainty method for multi-step retrieve-and-reason systems. It perturbs reasoning steps so that the retriever and generator reshape each other's inputs, capturing uncertainty from both. Reported: about 5% average AUROC gain over baselines on five systems, plus gains when used to abstain or to select a model.

## Relevance to the framework
Same line as L122, one step later: uncertainty for agentic, multi-step retrieval, which is closer to how an AI research assistant actually builds an argument. Useful mainly as a pointer to where ConfidAI is heading; a weak candidate for citation.
