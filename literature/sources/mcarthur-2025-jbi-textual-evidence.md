## Summary
JBI replaced its single text-and-opinion appraisal tool with three tools (narrative, expert opinion, policy), developed "using an adapted Delphi approach" by its Textual Evidence Methodology Group (abstract). The expert-opinion checklist is for reviewers appraising opinion texts for inclusion in systematic reviews.

## Key Findings
- Item 4: "Does the opinion demonstrate a logically defended argument to support the conclusions drawn?"; its guidance asks "Does the argument comply with Toulmin's model for argumentation?" (checklist; both FOUND by `tools/check_quotes`).
- Item 6 asks whether any incongruence with the literature or sources is logically defended.
- One overall question about the argument; no step-by-step check of evidence-to-claim links; no boundary-condition item.
