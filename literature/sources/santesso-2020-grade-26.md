# Santesso et al. 2020 — GRADE guidelines 26: informative statements (L98)

## Bibliographic Info
- **Authors:** Nancy Santesso, Claire Glenton, Philipp Dahm, et al.
- **Year:** 2020
- **Title:** GRADE guidelines 26: informative statements to communicate the findings of systematic reviews of interventions
- **Venue:** Journal of Clinical Epidemiology 119, 126–135
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.jclinepi.2019.10.014
- **Status:** PARTIAL (abstract via Europe PMC, 2026-10-16; not open access)

## Summary
Develops wording templates that combine the size of an effect with the certainty of the evidence, tested in workshops (80 attendees) and a survey of 110 producers and users of systematic reviews.

## Key Findings
- "Most participants agreed statements should be based on size of effect and certainty of evidence."
- For a large effect: high certainty "x results in a large reduction…"; moderate "x likely results in a large reduction…"; low "x may result in a large reduction…".
- "Statements for low, moderate and high certainty evidence were acceptable to >60%."
