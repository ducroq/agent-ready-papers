## Key Findings
- 14 tools; 54 documents in 6 classes. "Note that even the highest accuracy values are below 80%" (§5.1, Table 5); the best was Turnitin at 76%.
- Tools are "neither accurate nor reliable (all scored below 80% of accuracy and only 5 over 70%)" (§6).
- Human-written text: 96% average accuracy. Machine-translated human text: accuracy dropped by 20% (§5.1.1).
- Machine-paraphrased AI text: 26% overall accuracy. Human-edited AI text: 42% (§5.1.1).
- False accusations (Table 9): 6 of 14 tools produced false positives, averaging 2.4% on human text and 11.1% on machine-translated text. GPTZero's high false-positive share "makes this tool unsuitable for the academic environment".
- Reports "cannot be used as the only basis for reporting students for cheating" (§6).

## Caveats
- Very small n. Tested March–April 2023 against ChatGPT's February 2023 model.
- Tier **A** (peer-reviewed, empirical), weighted by the small sample.
