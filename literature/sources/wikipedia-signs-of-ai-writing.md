## What it is
An advice page for Wikipedia editors, framed as "descriptive, not prescriptive … observations, not rules". Its sign categories include:
- inflated significance ("testament", "pivotal moment")
- superficial trailing "-ing" analyses
- promotional language
- vague attributions ("experts argue")
- a formulaic challenges-and-outlook ending
- a high density of AI vocabulary
- avoiding is/are ("serves as")
- negative parallelisms ("not only … but", "it's not …, it's …")
- the rule of three
- formatting tells
- chatbot leftovers

## Self-stated limits (use these when citing it)
- Signs are "signs of a problem, not the problem itself". Do not treat them as the thing to fix, since "that could just make detection harder".
- It lists ineffective indicators: perfect grammar, academic prose, transition words on their own.
- The vocabulary shifts with each model generation; "delve" fell sharply in 2025.
- Detector tools have "non-trivial error rates".
