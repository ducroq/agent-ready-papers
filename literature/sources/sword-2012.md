## Key Findings (sample)
- Data: a survey of more than seventy academics, more than one hundred exemplary authors, one hundred writing guides (p. 9), and "one thousand peer-reviewed articles from sixty-six different journals in ten disciplines" (pp. 14–15), of which "five hundred articles (fifty from each discipline)" were analysed in detail (p. 15).
- What exemplary authors do (pp. 8–9): eye-catching titles; first-person anecdotes or asides; openings that ask a challenging question; concrete nouns and active verbs; numerous examples.
- "Vary your sentence length" (p. 3) is Sword *summarising* style guides, not her finding.
