## Summary
An editor's note to BAR's authors and reviewers on what makes a theoretical contribution. It summarises Whetten's (1989) four elements and then reproduces Whetten's closing questions as a checklist for reviewing a paper.

## Key Findings
- Addressed to reviewers: "BAR's authors and reviewers should carefully consider" Whetten's closing remarks "related to what constitutes a publishable paper".
- Uses those questions as a review instrument for a whole paper: "This is an interesting check list, but not the only one, to review a paper".
- Lists six of Whetten's seven questions (What's new? So what? Why so? Well done? Done well? Why now?), omitting "Who cares?".

## Relevance to agent-ready-papers
The only located source, other than Whetten himself, that explicitly recommends Whetten's criteria as a check applied to a paper's contribution as a whole, which is S3-2's proposition. As an editorial it does not meet DR-002's "peer-reviewed" bar on its own, and it supports Whetten's seven questions rather than the What/How/Why/Who-Where-When elements.
