## Key Findings
- 48 stories (12 New Yorker, 36 by GPT-3.5, GPT-4 and Claude 1.3 from the same plots). 10 experts, 14 binary tests (TTCW).
- Pass rates: New Yorker 84.7%, GPT-3.5 8.7%, GPT-4 27.9%, Claude 30.0% (Table 5). LLM judges do not agree with the experts.
- Expert testimony (qualitative, §5.6/§7.1): "predictable formations: strong topic sentences at the top of paragraphs; and summary sentences" at the end; repeated pet phrases; "overly moralizing" voice and endings.

## Caveats
- Formula features are testimony, not counts. One literary tradition, outdated models.
- Tier **B**.

## Relevance
- Expert readers name paragraph templates and moralising endings as failures. With L85 this suggests the formula penalty is an **expert-reader** effect.
