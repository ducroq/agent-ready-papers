# NASA Systems Engineering Handbook — verification vs validation (L97)

## Bibliographic Info
- **Authors:** NASA
- **Year:** 2016 (NASA/SP-2016-6105 Rev2; web edition)
- **Title:** NASA Systems Engineering Handbook, §2.0 Fundamentals of Systems Engineering
- **Venue:** NASA Special Publication
- **URL:** https://www.nasa.gov/reference/2-0-fundamentals-of-systems-engineering/
- **Status:** PARTIAL (the §2.0 web page only, fetched 2026-10-16)

## Summary
States the systems-engineering distinction the framework borrows: verification checks a product against its requirements; validation checks it against its intended purpose.

## Key Findings
- "Verification of a product shows proof of compliance with requirements"; "Verification testing relates back to the approved requirements set".
- "Validation of a product shows that the product accomplishes the intended purpose in the intended environment"; "Validation relates back to the ConOps document".
- The validation question: "Are we building the right product for our users and other stakeholders?"
