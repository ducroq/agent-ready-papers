## Key Findings
- "Experience shows that many authors, reviewers, and editors struggle to present and interpret DSR work well with a clear understanding of knowledge contributions." (p. 338)
- Conclusion: "Our goal is to establish some consensus on guidelines by which DSR research, as it is communicated to other scientists, can be judged." (p. 353)

## Relevance
Paper 1 S2-2: with Dinter & Krawatzeck 2015, one of two agreeing gap statements for design science (no consensus guidance on how DSR papers are presented and judged).

## Caveats
- **Partial counterexample.** It proposes a DSR knowledge contribution framework and a DSR publication schema (Table 3), which the authors call "guidelines; they are not meant to be followed blindly or serve as a straightjacket" (p. 352). An author proposal, not a consensus guideline.
