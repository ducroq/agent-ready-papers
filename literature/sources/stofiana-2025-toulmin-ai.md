## Summary
Convergent mixed-methods study of 30 final-year Indonesian EFL teacher-education students: argumentative essays analysed with Toulmin's model, metacognition with the MAI, and AI use with a questionnaire.

## Key Findings
- The essays' "argumentative quality analyzed through Toulmin’s model" (abstract).
- Elements receive scores: "Among Toulmin’s elements, Rebuttal scored lowest" (M = 1.87, SD = 0.43) (abstract).

## Relevance to agent-ready-papers
Probably a third instance of Toulmin elements scored as indicators of argument quality in student academic writing. It cannot be counted for S3-1 until the method section (rubric, raters, agreement) is read.
